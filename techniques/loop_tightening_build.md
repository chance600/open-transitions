## Spec

A grabbed loop (vocal/harmonic fragment) held over B's buildup while
progressively halved: stages of 4 / 2 / 1 / 1 bars over an 8-bar
build, the last stage looping a 1/2-bar slice. HPF cutoff rises
200 -> 500 -> 1200 -> 3000 Hz per stage; 1/4-beat fader chops ride
the loop throughout; the loop sits at -3 dB over B and releases with
a 1/2-bar fade so B's drop lands clean.

The loop is a constant the crowd can hold onto while its rate of
change accelerates — halving doubles the repetition rate, the rising
HPF strips its body, the chops turn it percussive. T-008's roll lives
inside A's last bar and T-020's glue loop is static; neither sustains
a tightening loop across B's build.
